Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Avincus Software Development Services ('we', 'our', or 'us') publishes the Marka mobile application ('the App'). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is protected.

In short: Marka works offline and your exam and student data stays on your device and is not uploaded to us. The only thing we keep about you is your Google sign-in account (see section 2).

2. Your Marka Account

Marka asks you to sign in with your Google account. We use it to recognise your account and to help protect Marka against unauthorised copies. When you sign in, Google and Firebase Authentication give us:

  • Your Google account ID and email address
  • The name and profile picture on your Google account
  • When you signed in

We also keep a small record for your account: the date it was created and the date you last used the App. These records contain no exam, student or score information, and are not used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting it removes your account and this record; your data on your device is not affected.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

When you use the App, the following is created and stored only on your device:

  • Subjects, exams, answer keys and MELC tags
  • Student names and IDs that you enter or import
  • Scanned answers, scores and analytics
  • Scanned answer sheet images
  • Backups you create

We do not have access to this data.

4. How Your Data Is Protected

  • The local database is encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher)
  • The encryption key is itself encrypted by a key held in the Android Keystore
  • Backups are encrypted with a password you choose. We cannot recover a forgotten password, and we cannot open your backups
  • Optional app lock using your fingerprint, face or device screen lock
  • Warnings on rooted or otherwise compromised devices

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium is purchased through Google Play. Google processes your payment and tells the App whether your subscription is active. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan answer sheets
  • Notifications (optional): to show reminders and status messages
  • Storage / file access: to save and share exports and backups you request
  • Biometrics or screen lock (optional): for the app lock feature

7. Third-Party Components

Marka uses Google ML Kit for text and barcode recognition. This recognition runs on your device. Images are not sent to us.

Marka uses Google Sign-In and Firebase (a Google service) to sign you in and to hold your account record. Their handling of that information is covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The App does not contain advertising and does not sell or share your data for marketing.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or share your student data. Files you export (CSV, PDF, backups) go only where you choose to send them.

We may disclose information only if required by law.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it
  • Deleted items remain in the in-app Recycle Bin for 30 days, then are removed
  • Uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device
  • Backups you export are copies you control; delete them yourself when no longer needed

Because we hold no copy of your exam data, you are in control of its deletion. We cannot recover data that you delete or lose, so please keep your own backups.

10. Children's Privacy

Marka is designed for educators (adults 18+). It stores information about students, who may be minors, that you enter. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you use Marka with students' information, you are responsible for:

  • Having the authority and any required consent from your school or guardians
  • Complying with applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012
  • Keeping your device secure

11. Your Rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you can view, correct, export and delete it directly in the App, and you can delete your account in Settings. For any other privacy request, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will change the 'Last Updated' date and, for significant changes, notify you in the App. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
