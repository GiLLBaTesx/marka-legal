Delete your Marka account

How to remove your account and what happens to your data

Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceDelete your account

You can delete your Marka account at any time, in the app or by emailing us.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Marka and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to choose your Google account once more.

Delete your account by email

If you can no longer open the app, email avincus.softwaredevelopment@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Marka account”. We will delete it within 48 hours and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Marka account (your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture as stored by sign-in)
  • The small record kept for your account (the date it was created and the date you last used the app)

What is not deleted

Your exams, students, scores and backups are stored only on your own phone and are never sent to us, so deleting your account does not remove them. To remove them, use Settings → Clear all data in the app, or uninstall Marka. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Questions

Email avincus.softwaredevelopment@gmail.com. We reply within 48 hours.
